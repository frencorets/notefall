About

Notefall is a free, open-source play-along game. It listens through your microphone, works out which note you are playing and scores your timing. Any melodic instrument works: a real ocarina, a recorder, a whistle, your voice, or a wind instrument inside another video game played through your speakers.

The built-in library only contains melodies that are in the public domain or were written for this project. Please only share songs you wrote, that are in the public domain, or that you have permission to share.

Notefall is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any video game company, and it uses no third-party game assets, music, characters or logos.

Your audio never leaves your device. Settings and your songs are stored only in this browser.
